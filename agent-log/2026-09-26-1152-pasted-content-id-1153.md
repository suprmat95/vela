## 8. The quota "guardian"
- It is the quota scheduler of RF-47 (spec RF-36/37). There is no separate "guardian" class. It has three parts:
  - **Pure rules** in `vela/domain/quota.py`: `try_acquire(w, cls, n, now, margin, reserve, purchase_waiting)` (L85). `QuotaClass.SYNC` is refused whenever `purchase_waiting` is true (L89-90). `SYNC` and `PURCHASE` are capped at `effective - reserve` (L30-32). `after_429` (L97) zeroes the remaining budget for the window.
  - **Store:** `repos.quota`, which is `PostgresQuota` (`vela/adapters/repo_postgres.py:343`, one row locked `FOR UPDATE`) or `MemoryQuota` (`repo_memory.py:195`).
  - **Calling pattern** in `JobProcessor` (`vela/domain/jobs.py:30-73`): `quota.acquire(cls, n, now)` before running; if refused, reschedule to `quota.next_window_start(now)`; on a 429 call `quota.on_429(now)`. `refresh_quota()` (L37) reads `/v1/quota` at boot and after a 429.
- **For a sync:** call `repos.quota.acquire(QuotaClass.SYNC, n, now, purchase_waiting=repos.jobs.purchase_waiting())`. `purchase_waiting()` exists on the jobs repo (`repo_postgres.py:324`, `repo_memory.py:176`). If refused, wait until `next_window_start`; on a 429 call `on_429`.
- `QuotaClass.SYNC` is already defined (`vela/domain/models.py:248-252`) and covered by tests (`tests/quota_contract.py:12`, `tests/test_quota_rules.py:27`), but no production code uses it yet.
- `scripts/api_explore.py:38` has a separate script-only `QuotaGuard(cap=90, ...)` with `sync`, `can_request`, `wait_seconds` and `record`. It is not part of the app.
